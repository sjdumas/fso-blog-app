# Welcome to Blog App

Blog App is a place to share and discover great blog posts from around the web. Find what other readers recommend, save posts for later, and keep track of what you've read.

## What you can do

- **Browse blogs** sorted by popularity on the [blogs page](/blogs)
- **Search** for blogs by title
- **Like** the blogs you enjoy, to help others find them
- **Save blogs** to your personal reading list
- **Generate an API token** to access your data from your own scripts and apps

## Getting started

1. [Create an account](/register).
2. [Log in](/login) with your new account.
3. Create your first blog post.
4. View other [users](/users) or [explore](/blogs) what others have shared.

---

Blog App is built with Next.js, TypeScript, Postgres, and Drizzle ORM as part of the [Full Stack Open](https://fullstackopen.com/en/) course.
